# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project should be able to contribute without
harassment or discrimination. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews
and other project spaces.

Be respectful, welcome different experience levels and backgrounds, and give
constructive feedback about the work rather than attacking the person.
Disagreement is welcome; intimidation, personal insults, discriminatory language,
sexual harassment, threats and publishing private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions or
restrict participation to protect the community. Responses should reflect the
severity and recurrence of the behaviour. Maintainers are expected to follow
the same standards.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact channel
listed on their [profile](https://github.com/digimangos). Do not publish private
details in an issue. Reports will be handled with discretion and shared only
as needed to address the concern.
